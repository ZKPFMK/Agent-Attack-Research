\section{Threat Model}
\subsection{Attacker and victim}
Our structured model is recorded in `THREAT-MODEL.md` and `threat/<variant>.json`. The attacker has <<knowledge>>
and can <<capabilities>> through <<access point>>; they cannot <<out of scope>>. The victim is <<model/framework,
topology, tools and memory>>.
\subsection{Success and controls}
Attack success is defined operationally as <<rule/canary/tool call/validated judge>>. We also measure benign
utility, false-trigger rate, detection/stealth and cost. The naive injection and no-attack arms isolate the
mechanism from a generally weak victim.
