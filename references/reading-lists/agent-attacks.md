# Agent-attack reading list

Use the user's KB first (`aa kb-search`) and then verify public papers with `paper-search`. These are search axes,
not claims of novelty or a substitute for reading the source.

## 01 external interaction

- web/content/evidence injection and indirect prompt injection
- chat-template and role confusion
- GUI/visual/pop-up and environment manipulation
- MCP/tool metadata, retrieval and selection hijacking
- toolchain consequences, resource/cost attacks
- public attack benchmarks and black-box red-team search

## 02 internal cognition

- training/model backdoors and action-level triggers
- reasoning hijacking, constraint tightening and malfunction amplification
- memory/RAG and multimodal memory poisoning
- privacy and knowledge-file extraction

## 03 multi-agent collaboration

- prompt infection and contagious jailbreaks
- communication/message tampering and hidden malicious agents
- role-driven collusion and covert channels
- compositional/distributed prompt attacks
- manipulated knowledge, trust/provenance and misinformation propagation
- malicious code/control flow and recursive blocking

## Defense and context

Read related defenses in 04–06, frameworks in 07, benchmarks in 08 and misinformation/trust work in 09. For each
entry record access point, knowledge, victim state, success criterion, topology, model/framework, n, judge and
defenses. Use `LITERATURE.md` and `KB-POSITION.md` for the dated audit trail.
